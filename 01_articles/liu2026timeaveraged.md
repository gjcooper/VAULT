## My Thoughts

A very mathematical treatment of the issues with some approximation methods for with trial time varying drift as well as across trial drift variability models. One particular model mentioned specifically is the [[attentional drift diffusion model]] as that has within trial effects on drift rate of attentional mechanisms (ie eye fixation increases drift)

A lot of the mathematics went over my head - but they do propose an alternative estimation method that does have good properties, and I particularly like the way they show recovered parameters with a specific shading where the incorrectly recovered values would lead to a different interpretation across groups.
