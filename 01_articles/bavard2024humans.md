## My Thoughts

An interesting test of how well people do at inferring social preferences when presented with no information, choice alone, RT alone and both choice and RT. There was some improvement in predictive power by participants of their opponents choices when observing just RT.

The task used throughout is the [[Dictator Game]], RT alone is modelled with the [[diffusion decision model]] (but integrated over both possible choices). Choice alone is modelled with a softmax decision rule, and social preference is estimated as a free parameter. Sequential decision making was modelled with either a [[reinforcement learning]] process, or via a [[Bayes-optimal model]] with full Bayesian inference.

Points of note.
- Observed more difficult decision taking more time in social domain (useful for [[Bayesian-Belief response time model]] work)
- [[Bayes-optimal model]] not as good as simpler [[reinforcement learning]] model unlike prior work.
- RT alone condition better than nothing, but choice + RT added nothing. Perhaps choices sufficient and response times not taken into account when choice information present.
