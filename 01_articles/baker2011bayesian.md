## My Thoughts

Part of my research for the [[Bayesian-Belief response time model]] paper, a good discussion about [[theory of mind]], and the way an agents beliefs can be represented as a distribution over a space.

This paper (although short) seems to be part of some quite influential work that introduces the idea of theory of mind being the integration of a joint model of another agents desires (goals) and their beliefs (their current understanding of their environment).

Here Baker et. al. apply their insights to a task where the subject has to infer the preferences of another agent based on their behaviour. Specifically if the other agent shows signs of preferring one cuisine over another based on their exploration (looking around a corner - see example stimulus)
