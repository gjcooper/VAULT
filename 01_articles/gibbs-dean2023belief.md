## My Thoughts

An attempt to synthesise across various belief updating work, where beliefs in this case are generally beliefs about environmental reward/volatility structures. This includes tasks such as [[reversal learning]] taks, [[stochasticity vs volatility]] tasks and similar.

There are various models considered, bu most articles reviewed looked at [[reinforcement learning]] with either single or dual learning rates, and [[heirarchical gaussian filter]] models. There was some discussion of other models included.

Finally the reviews were restricted to studies of clinical (or sub-clinical) populations, including [[depression]], [[schizophrenia]] (and other psychosis clinical groups) and [[anxiety]].

All told there was mixed evidence for consistent effects on learning rates and related parameters for most groups except for psychosis groups.

Some nice citations of [[pike2022reinforcement]] towards the end, that indicate possibilities in creating more comparable studies/model fits.
