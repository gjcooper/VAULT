## My Thoughts

Some nice work questioning thge assumptions of pure single architecture/stopping rule approaches of applying [[Systems Factorial Technology|SFT]] to decision making. That is - what happens if people use a mixture of different approaches.

Through simulation studies they show that there can be significant [[model mimicry]] for certain mixtures in aspects like the Survivor Interaction Contrast. To combat this they introduce a hierarchical Bayesian mixture model and show that under certain assumptions (that errors are caused by decision rule-breaking) the generating mixtures can be recovered even when there is a mixture of both architecture and stopping rule.

Did I use this in the [[Architecture of MultiAttribute Choice]] paper? I hope so.
