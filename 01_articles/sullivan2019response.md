## My Thoughts

A very snarky sounding reply to a critique of an original paper about using [[mouse trajectory]] measurements to infer attribute weights in a healthy eating paradigm.

I did like the use of simulations of the assumptions in the critique to show that the critique does not necessarily show what Zhang et al (2018) believe.
